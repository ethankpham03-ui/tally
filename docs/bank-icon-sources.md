# Banking app icon sources

Retrieved and checked on 7 September 2026.

Tally bundles the original **App Store application icons** for 32 banks: 13 Vietnam, 9 United States, 10 United Kingdom. Cash uses the original [Apple Wallet app icon](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-wallet/id1160481993), published by Apple Inc.. These are the bank apps' square artwork, including the app's own background and any current seasonal treatment. No website favicons, extracted brand symbols, synthesized monograms, or redrawn Wallet artwork are used.

All 33 assets are original 512×512 JPEGs downloaded from Apple's artwork CDN and stored in `public/banks/`. CSS only rounds the corners for display; it does not recolor the artwork. Account names and bank matching stay in the browser. There is no runtime request to an external logo service.

This is an initial catalog, not an exhaustive bank directory. App names/publishers and regional editions were checked: for example TD Bank (US), HSBC UK, Santander UK and Royal Bank of Scotland. Multinational bank icons can be selected for accounts in any currency. Banks' names and app artwork remain their owners' property; their display identifies the user's source and does not imply affiliation.

## Updating the catalog

1. Identify the bank's official retail banking application in the appropriate App Store region. Verify the publisher and bank website when a service provider publishes the app.
2. Download the original app artwork from the verified App Store record. Keep its full square image, original colors and proportions. Do not substitute a website favicon or standalone corporate mark.
3. Save the asset in `public/banks/`, update the matching `BANKS` entry and record the App Store page, artwork URL, publisher and hash in the provenance JSON.
4. Run catalog tests and inspect cash/bank icons on both light and dark account surfaces. Unknown banks and failed asset loads use the generic Phosphor icon; an explicit `bankId` overrides automatic whole-word name matching.
